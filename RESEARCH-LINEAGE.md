# ANNE Research Lineage

## Status: Foundational Core Layer — Active Development

`anne-core` is the **first foundational/core engine layer of the current ANNE development direction**.

The lineage is intentionally separated into three roles:

```text
anne
  → first/main ANNE line
  → FROZEN / historical reference

anne-ai
  → current ANNE research platform
  → active architectural development

anne-core
  → first foundational/core engine layer
  → active development of reusable, installable core capabilities
```

### Relationship between the three repositories

The three repositories form **one ANNE research lineage with three distinct roles**.

- **`anne`** is the original first/main ANNE development line and is frozen as a historical research snapshot.
- **`anne-ai`** is the current canonical research platform where the broader architecture, integration work, experiments, and research milestones are developed.
- **`anne-core`** is the first foundational/core engine layer of that current direction. Capabilities that can be isolated, tested, reused, and installed independently are developed here alongside `anne-ai`.

The practical relationship is:

```text
Original ANNE research line
        │
        ▼
     anne
   FROZEN
        │
        │ architectural continuation
        ▼
   anne-ai  ◄──────────────►  anne-core
 CURRENT PLATFORM            FOUNDATIONAL CORE
 active research             reusable core layer
```

`anne-ai` and `anne-core` are therefore complementary rather than competing repositories. Platform-level architecture and integration remain associated with `anne-ai`; foundational reusable capabilities are developed and extracted through `anne-core`.
